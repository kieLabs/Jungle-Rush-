📖 Explorer Survival Guide

📱 Mobile Touch Controls

• Swipe Left / Right: Switch between the 3 temple lanes.
• Swipe Up: Jump over low barriers, spikes, gaps & lava.
• Swipe Down: Slide under ancient gates, branches & pendulums.
• Double Tap: Activate Hero Special Ability.

⌨️ Desktop Keyboard Controls

• Left / A & Right / D: Switch lanes smoothly.
• Up / W / Space: Jump.
• Down / S: Slide.
• E / Shift: Trigger Hero Special Ability.
• Escape / P: Pause Game.

💎 Currencies & Endless Survival

There is NO distance limit and NO final level. Run through 5 cycling biomes (Jungle, Waterfall Gorge, Lava Caverns, Sunken Ruins, Golden Sanctum). Collect Gold Coins, Magic Gems, and Ancient Tokens to unlock all 8 Heroes and Level 10 Upgrades!
